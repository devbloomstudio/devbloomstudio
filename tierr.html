TIERR

A collection launch, from the atelier to the coast.

TIERR is a fashion label that makes its pieces in its own atelier. We created the content for the launch of their new collection: detail photography and footage inside the atelier, where every piece is cut, sewn and labelled, and a series of films shot outdoors on location, by the sea, in gardens and at sunset, from the first “available soon” teaser to the launch itself.

Services

Content Creation · Art Direction · Fashion Photography · Video Production · Reels · Social Media Content

In the atelier we stayed close to the details that show how carefully each piece is made: the woven labels, the branded hangers, the fabric and the sewing machine.

Outside, warm natural light, the sea and slow, cinematic moves give the collection room to breathe, keeping the focus on the clothes from the first frame to the last.

Lookbook
