SAN MARTINO 26

San Martino 26 is a trattoria moderna built on simple flavours, fresh produce and handmade pasta. We run their social media, from post design to photography and reels, with a feed that moves between the plate, the kitchen and the dining room.

Services

Social Media Strategy · Creative Direction · Content Planning · Design · Photography · Reels

Warm, grainy photography meets a quiet serif wordmark and the hand-drawn trattoria mark, so every post feels like a page from the restaurant’s own menu.

Dish posts put the pasta front and centre, from cappellacci in broth to spaghetti al pomodoro on a bed of sliced tomatoes, while black-and-white portraits bring the chef and the kitchen into the story.

Posts

Feed designs for dishes, the team, the dining room and the trattoria’s philosophy.

Photography

Dishes, service and the kitchen, photographed in the trattoria.

Reels

Short, playful reels from the kitchen counter.
