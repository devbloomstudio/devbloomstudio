SELECT WATCHES

Select Watches is a multi-brand watch store bringing together names like G-Shock, Ice-Watch, Swiss Military, Cover, Festina and Fossil. We run their social media season by season: bold product campaigns for summer, a playful back-to-school sale, and a return-to-the-office series, supported by our own photo and video shoots that put the watches on real wrists, by the pool and on the tennis court.

Services

Social Media Strategy · Creative Direction · Content Planning · Post Design · Photo & Video Production · Reels

Each campaign gives the watches a world of their own: underwater, on a forest trail, in the sky or through a keyhole, always with the Select Watches mark close by.

A clean grey base, navy type and a grainy film texture hold very different brands together, so the feed feels like one store with one point of view.

Feed

Posts, photography, campaign videos and reels created for Select Watches’ Instagram.
