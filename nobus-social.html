NOBUS

Nobus Hotel & Spa sits above the sea, with an infinity pool that seems to run straight into the horizon. We run their social media around that view and the feeling of a slow summer: mornings with breakfast on the terrace or floating in the pool, long afternoons by the water, sushi and cocktails at golden hour, and quiet evenings in the spa. Our own photo and video shoots capture the hotel in its best light, in soft, sun-washed tones of turquoise, sand and sunset.
