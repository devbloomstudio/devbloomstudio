About us

↓ Mission & vision

We are a strategic content and creative studio built around a strong belief in aesthetics, quality and purpose. Founded in 2021, Bloom Studios grew from a passion in photography into a multidisciplinary studio where visual storytelling, creative direction and digital strategy come together.

We partner with brands to create distinctive identities, meaningful content and digital experiences designed not only to look good, but to perform.

We're always drawn to ambitious ideas, new challenges and brands ready to grow.

Will yours be next?

Mission

To build digital presences that feel distinctive, relevant and impossible to overlook.

We combine strategy, creativity and high quality content to help brands communicate with clarity, connect with the right audience and grow with purpose.

Vision

To shape brands that stand out visually & strategically. We believe the strongest brands are built where storytelling, design and strategy meet.

Our vision is to create work that feels authentic, performs with purpose and builds lasting value beyond the feed.

For Miell Tirana, we reimagined the identity of one of Albania's established flour producers, evolving it from a traditional image into a cleaner, more contemporary and professional brand. The rebrand reflects the company's growth and its focus on quality, reliability, technology and international standards.

Services

Rebranding · Creative Direction · Logo Design · Visual Identity · Typography & Color System · Illustration Direction · Packaging · Brand Applications

Miell Tirana

A new identity for a brand rooted in tradition and built for what comes next.

At the heart of the new identity is a distinctive symbol combining the letterform with wheat — connecting the brand directly to its product and heritage while giving it a stronger, more recognizable presence. An earthy palette of greens, wheat-inspired neutrals and warm golden tones reinforces the connection to grain and origin.

The system extends beyond the logo into typography, illustration, packaging, corporate materials and large-scale applications, creating a flexible identity that bridges Miell Tirana's heritage with a more modern future.
